Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about WealthWise.

Is WealthWise free to use?

WealthWise offers a free tier with basic features that's perfect for getting started with personal finance management. Our Pro and Business plans offer advanced features for those who need more powerful tools.

How secure is my financial data?

We take security very seriously. All data is encrypted using industry-standard encryption protocols, and we never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. You'll continue to have access to your paid features until the end of your billing period.

Does WealthWise work on mobile devices?

Yes! WealthWise is fully responsive and works on all devices. We also have native mobile apps for iOS and Android that you can download from the App Store or Google Play Store.
